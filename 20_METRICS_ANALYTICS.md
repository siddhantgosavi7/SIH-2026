# Analytics and Institutional Intelligence

## 1. Operational dashboard

Show:

- complaints by category;
- active backlog;
- SLA compliance;
- average/median resolution time;
- routing accuracy;
- escalation count;
- reopen rate;
- duplicate cluster count.

## 2. Institutional insight

The long-term value is identifying systemic issues.

Example:

```text
Issue cluster: Hostel water supply
Reports: 42
Affected students: 310
Average response: 3h
Resolution: 19h
Repeated: 4 incidents this month
```

This turns individual complaints into evidence for administrative improvement.

## 3. Fairness and quality checks

Monitor whether:

- certain departments receive systematically lower AI confidence;
- particular categories are over-prioritized;
- manual overrides are concentrated in a category;
- SLA breaches are caused by routing errors;
- duplicate detection misses recurring issues.

## 4. Judge-facing metric story

Do not say:

> "Our AI is 99% accurate."

Instead say:

> "We evaluate the system at each decision point: classification, duplicate detection, routing, SLA compliance, and human override rate."

This sounds more credible and is easier to defend.
