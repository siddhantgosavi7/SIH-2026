# MVP Roadmap

## Must-have for hackathon demo

- student authentication;
- student dashboard;
- complaint submission;
- AI classification;
- semantic duplicate/related detection;
- explainable priority;
- routing to at least Exam Cell, Hostel, Academics/HOD;
- department dashboard;
- assignment;
- SLA timer;
- escalation;
- resolution;
- student validation;
- reopen;
- central history;
- audit timeline.

## Should-have

- analytics dashboard;
- recurring issue clusters;
- configurable SLA;
- configurable routing policy;
- notification templates;
- attachment handling.

## Could-have

- multilingual support;
- voice input;
- predictive trend analysis;
- capacity-aware routing;
- knowledge-base recommendations.

## Do not prioritize

- complex microservices;
- custom model training;
- blockchain;
- unnecessary mobile app if web is sufficient;
- decorative AI features with no measurable value.

## Demo sequence

1. Login as student.
2. Submit a realistic complaint.
3. Show AI category and priority explanation.
4. Show duplicate detection using a second complaint.
5. Show department routing.
6. Switch to department dashboard.
7. Show SLA timer and assignment.
8. Resolve the complaint.
9. Show notification/validation.
10. Validate or reopen.
11. Show audit timeline.
12. Show analytics demonstrating the institutional insight created by aggregation.
